# Cyclone Amphan: context figures

Background figures collected by the analysis team for storytelling. No sources
were recorded with them, and several are ranges, so none of these are shown in
the app yet. Add a source next to a figure before using it on screen.

Sources were asked for on 16 November 2026 together with the map layers. The
layers, grid corners and histogram arrived; sources for these figures did not.

## Key flood and impact statistics

- Landfall date: 20 May 2020
- Peak wind speeds: 240–270 km/h (150–165 mph)
- Total fatalities: about 133–158+ across India and Bangladesh (about 72–95 in India and 20–26+ in Bangladesh)
- People evacuated: over 4 million (about 2.4–2.6 million in Bangladesh and over 1 million in West Bengal and Odisha)
- Economic losses: estimated 13.2 to 15.5 billion USD

## India (West Bengal and Odisha)

- Inundated area: over 22,735 km² flooded across the West Bengal and Odisha coast, heavily affecting the Sundarbans and South 24 Parganas (for example Namkhana about 198.5 km², Kultali about 187.3 km²)
- East Midnapore: about 52,037 hectares of flooded region identified, destroying 9,575 hectares of crops
- Housing and infrastructure: over 2.9 million homes damaged or destroyed in India; Kolkata Airport suffered severe runway and hangar flooding

## Bangladesh

- Inundated area: over 4,000 km² inundated by storm surges and breached embankments across coastal districts such as Khulna, Barisal and Patuakhali
- Affected population: about 2.6 to 10 million people across 19 to 26 districts
- Infrastructure and agriculture: over 205,000 houses damaged, 55,000 destroyed, and 32,037 hectares of crops and vegetables and 18,707 hectares of fish cultivation areas washed out
